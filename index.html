Your first visit

No need to know which massage you want. We'll work it out together.

  1. Book

    Call or message 598 300 198 with a day and time that suits you.

  2. Arrive

    Come 10 minutes early to settle in. Wear whatever is comfortable.

    Tell us where it hurts, any injuries, and how firm you like the pressure.

  4. Unwind

    Enjoy the session, then leave with a few simple stretches for home.
